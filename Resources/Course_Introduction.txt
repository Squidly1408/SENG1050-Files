Academic Integrity
As a student at UON, you must strive to adhere to the academic integrity policies and requirements of the University. The University takes any academic misconduct or dishonesty, such as plagiarism, very seriously, and a range of actions and penalties might be taken in response to any misconduct. Therefore, it is very important to become familiar with what is expected of you and the policies that are in place. You can find further information from https://policies.newcastle.edu.au/document/view-current.php?id=35Links to an external site.

New students in their first semester/trimester are required to complete an Academic Integrity Module before the end of the semester/trimester. This online module helps you become familiar with the University's five values of academic integrity that you must uphold while you're a student at UON, as well as reduce plagiarism and academic fraud.

You can access the module and related information about it through this link: https://www.newcastle.edu.au/current-students/study-essentials/assessment-and-exams/academic-integrity/academic-integrity-moduleLinks to an external site.
