XML

Extensible Markup Language (XML) is a markup language that defines a set of rules for encoding documents in a format that is both human-readable and machine-readable. The W3C's XML 1.0 Specification and several other related specifications—all of them free open standards—define XML.

  • https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/XML
  • https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/thumb/6/68/XML.svg/300px-XML.svg.png
Originally designed to meet the challenges of large-scale electronic publishing, XML is also playing an increasingly important role in the exchange of a wide variety of data on the Web and elsewhere.
